Bibliography

The reference books behind this book.

This book is positioned as a runnable, opinionated reference that points at a small set of excellent books rather than displacing them. Chapter-by-chapter cross-references land here as chapters are written; for now, the reading list.

Citation discipline

Per the project's editorial constraint: every citation in this book is a pointer — "see Williams ch. 4 for the full pattern" — not a displacing summary. The books exist; if this book replaced reading them, it would be doing the authors and the reader a disservice.
